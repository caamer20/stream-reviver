NAME
Stream Reviver

SUMMARY
Detect and safely recover failed live HTML5 streams on sites you explicitly enable.

CATEGORIES
Other, Tabs

DATA COLLECTION
Required: none. Stream Reviver does not transmit analytics, technical data, browsing activity, or personal information.

REVIEW NOTES
Use docs/REVIEWER_GUIDE.md and the generated local fixture. The MV3 Gecko manifest declares data_collection_permissions.required = ["none"]. Optional site access is requested one origin at a time.
