NAME
Stream Reviver

SUMMARY
Detect stalled live video, recover conservatively, and stop refresh loops—only on sites you explicitly enable.

CATEGORY
Productivity

LANGUAGE
English (en)

PRIVACY DISCLOSURES
No data is sold or transmitted externally by extension code. Settings, enabled origins, selectors, diagnostics, and local models remain in browser extension storage. Optional visual sampling is processed ephemerally in the privileged background; only a fixed comparison hash reaches the requesting content script and no image bytes are stored or returned. Publish docs/PRIVACY.md at HTTPS before submission.

PERMISSION JUSTIFICATION
storage: local/synced settings and bounded recovery state.
activeTab: explicit user controls and opt-in active-tab visual samples.
scripting: per-origin dynamic content-script registration after consent.
alarms: one-shot recovery/countdown deadlines that survive MV3 service-worker suspension; no browsing access.
notifications: optional loop-protection warnings.
optional host access: exact origins selected by the user; no persistent all-sites grant.
