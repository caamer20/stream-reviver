Stream Reviver

A conservative, opt-in safety net for live HTML5 video streams.

  1. Enable one site.The browser grants access only to the origin you choose.
  2. Leave the stream playing.Conservative checks distinguish a sustained failure from ordinary buffering or a pause.
  3. Stay in control.Recovery starts with soft actions, shows a cancelable reload countdown, and stops at a circuit breaker.

Before enabling monitoring

No analytics, tracking, or external API calls. Settings remain in browser storage.
