Understand what the extension can do

Stream Reviver monitors only sites you explicitly enable. It starts with conservative, low-disruption actions and protects every page reload with limits and a cancelable countdown.

Local by designNo analytics, tracking, or external API calls.

Your safety net is ready

Open a stream, enable its site from the toolbar, and leave Stream Reviver to monitor conservatively.

  • The toolbar popup shows current health and the next planned action.
  • A page reload always has a countdown you can cancel.
  • Settings includes per-site rules, diagnostics, and complete local-data deletion.

No stream content is stored or transmitted. Browser fullscreen restrictions still apply.
